<h2>Project Overview</h2>
 
This project represents a collection of SQL business analyses developed during a Data Analytics Internship to support executive reporting and operational decision-making for the Food & Beverage department of a performing arts nonprofit.
 
The Food & Beverage department is the organization's highest revenue-generating division. Using live organizational data stored in Snowflake, I transformed raw transactional data into meaningful insights that supported executive-level reporting through The Bar Report.
 
The goal was to evaluate performance trends, identify top-performing products and events, and support weekly business decision-making.
 
<h2>Business Objective</h2>
 
The Food & Beverage department required consistent, data-driven insights to answer:
 - Which <b>items and categories drive the most revenue?</b>
 - Which <b>events and genres perform best in food sales?</b>
 - How do <b>profitability and pricing vary across items?</b>
 - What trends should leadership monitor weekly?

 <h2>📊 SALES ANALYSIS: By Item Sales</h2>
 
**SQL Query:** [By Item Sales Query](SQL_Queries/By_Item_Sales_Query.sql)
 
<b>Business Scenario</b>
 
Business leaders wanted to identify which food and beverage products generated the highest sales and profit margins across entertainment events.
 
<b>Project Overview</b>
 
This query provides an item-level sales summary for every product sold during an event, along with the corresponding profit margin for a specified time period.
 
<b>Business Value</b>
 
- Evaluate product profitability
- Identify high-performing and underperforming products
- Optimize pricing strategies
- Improve inventory planning
- Maximize food and beverage revenue
 
<br />
 
 <h2>🎟 EVENT PERFORMANCE: By Transaction</h2>
 
**SQL Query:** [By Transaction Query](SQL_Queries/By_Transaction_Query.sql)
 
<b>Business Scenario</b>
 
Leadership wanted to compare customer purchasing activity across events to determine which entertainment performances generated the greatest transaction volume.
 
<b>Project Overview</b>
 
This query returns the total number of transactions for each event, along with the corresponding entertainment genre, for a specified date range.
 
<b>Business Value</b>
 
- Measure customer engagement
- Compare event popularity
- Analyze genre performance
- Identify high-performing events
- Support scheduling and marketing decisions
 
<br />
 
 <h2>🎤 EXECUTIVE REPORTING: Top Event by Genre</h2>
 
**SQL Query:** [Top Event by Genre Query](SQL_Queries/Top_Event_by_Genre_Query.sql)
 
<b>Business Scenario</b>
 
Executives wanted to determine the highest-performing event within each entertainment genre to better understand audience demand.
 
<b>Project Overview</b>
 
This multi-page query identifies the top-performing event for each of the company's 21 entertainment genres—including Country, Rock/Pop, Comedy, Family, and more—for a specified date range.
 
<b>Business Value</b>
 
- Compare event performance across all genres
- Identify revenue-generating events
- Support future booking decisions
- Improve promotional planning
- Enhance revenue forecasting
 
<br />
 
 <h2>🍿 PRODUCT ANALYTICS: Top Genre by Category</h2>
 
**SQL Query:** [Top Genre by Category Query](SQL_Queries/Top_Genre_by_Category_Query.sql)
 
<b>Business Scenario</b>
 
Food and beverage managers wanted to determine which concession items performed best within each department category based on the genre of entertainment being presented.
 
<b>Project Overview</b>
 
This multi-page query identifies the top-selling item within a selected food and beverage department category and correlates those sales with the genre of the associated entertainment event.
 
<b>Business Value</b>
 
- Optimize inventory allocation
- Improve product assortment
- Increase concession revenue
- Reduce product shortages
- Support purchasing decisions

 <h2>💰 EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD: Sales & Collections</h2>
